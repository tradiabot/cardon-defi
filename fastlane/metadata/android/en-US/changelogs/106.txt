New: "Real-time status" in Settings. A traffic light checks the cloud, cycles, networks, AI, funds and swaps. "Find and fix" applies only safe fixes (rerun a cycle, reorder AI models) and the AI explains what remains; anything that changes your setup, like moving a market to simulation, asks for confirmation. The agent reorders the AI by itself when it fails, and the app notifies you when a market keeps failing for more than one cycle.
